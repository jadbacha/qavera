THE BEGINNING

Chocolate is more
than chocolate.

Qavera began with a simple belief: chocolate is an art and an experience.

We wanted to create something that went beyond ordinary chocolate — something that could be admired before it was tasted, discovered with every bite, and remembered long after.

Every Qavera creation is made with intention, combining premium artisan chocolate, refined craftsmanship, and unique flavors.

THE NAME

A name built
on authenticity.

Qavera is a name that reflects who we are and where we come from.

QA represents Qatar, the home of Qavera.

Vera means authentic in Latin.
Together, the name represents an authentic chocolate house founded and based in Qatar.
