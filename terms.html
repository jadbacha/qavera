How ordering from QAVERA works: delivery, payment, cancelling, and what happens if something goes wrong.

Last updated: October 2026

Orders & prices

All prices are in Qatari Riyals (QAR). The price you pay is the price shown when you place your order. Products marked "Sold out" can't be ordered until they are back in stock.

Box colours, chocolate colours, finishes and designs may differ slightly from the photos. Every piece is made by hand.

Dates & times

  • Premium and Luxury boxes are made to order, so the earliest delivery or pickup is the next day.
  • Other products can be delivered or collected the same day, if you order at least 1 hour before your time slot starts.
  • Delivery times: 12 PM – 4 PM or 6 PM – 11 PM. Pickup times: 10 AM – 2 PM or 4 PM – 9 PM (Fridays: 1 PM – 6 PM). Orders can be scheduled up to 30 days ahead.

Delivery & pickup

We deliver across Qatar for QAR 20. Pickup from the QAVERA shop is free.

Our driver will call you on arrival. If we can't reach you or nobody is available to receive the order, we can deliver it again at another time for a new delivery fee of QAR 20, or you can collect it from the shop.

Payment

You pay by cash or card when your order is delivered or when you collect it. We never ask for or store card details on this website.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel for free until we confirm your order. Once it is confirmed, we start preparing it and it can no longer be cancelled. To cancel, contact us by WhatsApp or phone as soon as possible.

If something is wrong

If your order arrives damaged, or an item is wrong or missing, please contact us within 24 hours with a photo, and we will replace the item.

Because our products are food, we can't accept returns or offer refunds for change of mind.

Storage & allergens

Keep chocolate in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight and heat.

Our products are made in a kitchen that handles nuts, milk, gluten and soy, and may contain traces of them. If you have an allergy, please contact us before ordering.

QAVERA points

Signed-in customers earn 100 points for every QAR 1,000 spent on products. Points are added when the order is delivered, and every 100 points gives QAR 10 off a future order. Points have no cash value. If an order is cancelled, points earned on it are removed and points you used on it are returned.
