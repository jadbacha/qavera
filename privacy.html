Privacy

Your privacy.

What information we keep when you shop with QAVERA, and why.

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, email address and phone number. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google. Passwords are handled by our secure sign-in provider; we never see them.
  • Your orders: the products you order, your delivery address or pickup choice, your chosen date and time, and any notes you add.
  • Saved addresses: addresses you choose to save for faster checkout.
  • Points: your QAVERA points balance.
  • Messages: anything you send us through the contact form.

How we use it

Only to run QAVERA: to prepare and deliver your orders, to contact you about them, to send you order emails, to keep your points, and to answer your messages.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it with anyone except where needed to deliver your order.

Where it is kept

Your information is stored securely with our database provider, and order emails are sent through our email provider. Only the QAVERA team can see your orders.

Payments

You pay by cash or card on delivery or at pickup. This website never asks for or stores card details.

Cookies & storage

We only use your browser's storage for what the site needs to work: keeping you signed in and remembering your cart. We do not use advertising trackers.

Your choices

You can update your name and phone number in your account at any time. To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to close your account, contact us and we will help.
